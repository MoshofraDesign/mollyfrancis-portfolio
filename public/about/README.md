## Sizing tips

- Aim for **1500–2000px** on the long edge, JPEG, ~70–80% quality. Next.js will resize automatically.
- Square or near-square crops work best in the polaroid layouts.
- The `pet-bed.jpg`, `collect-pens.jpg`, and `love-coffee.jpg` photos render larger — give them more pixels (2400+).

## File format

`.jpg` is fine for photos. `.png` works for anything with transparency.
